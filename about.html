About

Tahir Ali, electronic product development engineer

An individual engineer who takes your electronic product from idea to complete, editable engineering files.

Who you will be working with

I'm Tahir Ali, a freelance engineer working with clients remotely from Pakistan. TA Embedded Solutions is my own practice: there is no agency, no team and no outsourcing layer. You talk to me, and I do the work.

I got into electronics by designing boards, and I also study Electrical Engineering at Namal University, Mianwali (BSc, 2023 to 2027). Since then, my work with clients on Fiverr, Upwork and Freelancer.com has grown from PCB layout into the whole path a product takes.

How I approach product development

I make the product work first: firmware on real hardware, tested and debugged on a breadboard. Then I design it professionally: a KiCad schematic, a checked PCB layout and an enclosure built around the real board. Each stage is reviewed with you before the next begins.

Engineering and hardware background

My hardware and firmware work covers ESP32 (C3, S3, C6), STM32 and Arduino/ATmega328P platforms, with Embedded C/C++, UART, SPI, I2C and interrupt-driven control. PCB work is mainly in KiCad and Altium Designer, with EasyEDA Pro as well. My largest board is an 8-layer layout with more than 400 components.

Documentation

I treat documentation as part of the product: organised project folders, schematic PDFs, wiring diagrams, testing notes, BOMs and manufacturing files, so another engineer or manufacturer can pick the project up without me.
