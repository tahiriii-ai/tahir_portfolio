01Project Objective & Engineering Challenge

A self-contained ESP32 platform that other firmware projects can be built on: USB Type-C for power and data, on-board regulation to 3.3 V, and the module's core interfaces exposed on GPIO headers.

02Technical Approach

  • Boot and reset push-buttons use RC filtering for debounce.
  • Ground plane poured solid under the RF section, with a dedicated keep-out zone around the module antenna.
  • ESD protection on the USB data lines (USBLC6-2SC6) and a 3.3 V AP2112K-1.2 LDO on board.
  • UART, SPI and I2C broken out across full GPIO headers.
