01Project Objective & Engineering Challenge

A small bridge board with a USB Type-C receptacle on the host side and a 5-pin header on the output, keeping the footprint compact while protecting the data lines and showing traffic on TX/RX LEDs.

02Technical Approach

  • CP2102 bridge chip with ESD protection diodes on both USB data lines.
  • TX and RX status LEDs for a visual read on traffic.
  • 100k / 101k resistor network in the signal path.
  • Header pinout: 3.3 V / 5 V / TX / RX / GND.
