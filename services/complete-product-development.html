Service 01

Complete Product Development

A firmware-first workflow that takes your idea through breadboard-tested hardware, a professional KiCad schematic and PCB, and a custom 3D enclosure, all handled by one engineer.

Discuss Your ProjectSee what you receive

Overview

You bring the product idea. I take care of the engineering workflow behind it: choosing the components, writing the firmware, proving the circuit on real hardware, and only then designing the schematic, PCB and enclosure.

Because the function is validated before a board is drawn, the PCB is designed around behaviour that already works. That is the idea behind my approach: first, I make your product work. Then, I design it professionally.

What you can bring me

  • A product idea
  • A rough sketch
  • Reference images
  • Functional requirements
  • An existing concept
  • Technical specifications, if you have them
How it works

The firmware-first workflow

  1. 01

    Understand Your Idea

    I review your idea, sketch, reference images or specification, then select components and a microcontroller and plan the hardware architecture.

  2. 02

    Develop Firmware

    I write the firmware for the chosen controller (ESP32, STM32 or Arduino) so the product behaviour exists before any board does.

  3. 03

    Build Hardware Prototype

    I assemble the circuit on a breadboard with real components: microcontroller, sensors, peripherals and power, then load the firmware.

  4. 04

    Test and Validate

    I test the real hardware and firmware together, debug integration problems and verify sensors, actuators and communication before a PCB is drawn.

  5. 05

    Design KiCad Schematic

    With the function proven, I draw the professional schematic in KiCad: component selection, symbols, footprints and a BOM.

  6. 06

    Develop PCB Layout

    I convert the approved schematic into a placed and routed board with design-rule and electrical-rule checks, copper zones and fabrication outputs.

  7. 07

    Design Custom Enclosure

    I model the housing around the real PCB: mounting, connector openings, display and button cutouts, with STEP and STL exports.

  8. 08

    Deliver Engineering Files

    You receive the editable project files and documentation, organised so your chosen manufacturers can build from them.

Can you develop an electronic product from just an idea?

Yes. You can start with an idea, a rough sketch, reference images, functional requirements or a full specification. I analyse the requirements, select the microcontroller and components, plan the hardware architecture and then take the product through firmware, breadboard prototype, testing, schematic, PCB layout and enclosure.

What is your product development workflow?

Eight stages: (1) idea and requirements, (2) firmware development, (3) breadboard hardware, (4) testing and debugging, (5) KiCad schematic, (6) PCB design, (7) custom 3D enclosure, and (8) engineering handover. You can see it on the process section and in Complete Product Development.

Who supplies the hardware components?

You do. Clients either supply the components or cover the purchasing cost after approval. I do not purchase components at my own expense, and I do not ship physical products.

Need complete product development?

Describe your product or send the requirements. I will review them and come back with the next step.
