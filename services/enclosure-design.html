Service 05

3D Enclosure Design

A custom housing designed around your real PCB: mounting, connector openings, display and button cutouts, delivered as editable CAD with STEP and STL exports.

Discuss Your ProjectSee what you receive

Overview

The enclosure is designed after the PCB, so it fits the board and its components properly instead of forcing the board to fit a box. I work from the exact PCB model and your product requirements.

Parts are modelled in Fusion 360, checked for clearance and interference, and exported as STEP and STL so they can be printed or sent to a manufacturer of your choice.

What you can bring me

  • The PCB, ideally as a STEP model
  • Battery or other component sizes
  • How the product is used and mounted
  • Look and finish requirements
How it works

What enclosure design includes

  1. 01

    Product housing

    Custom enclosure modelling for your product: shell, lid and any clips, retainers or brackets.

  2. 02

    PCB fitting

    Housing built around the actual PCB outline and mounting holes, so the board seats without extra brackets where possible.

  3. 03

    Mounting points

    Screw posts and mounting arrangements matched to the board, with fastener choices kept simple.

  4. 04

    Component placement

    Clearances for tall parts, batteries, LEDs, buzzers and antennas, including keeping metal away from RF areas.

  5. 05

    Connector cutouts

    USB and other connector openings, display and button cutouts and vents positioned from the real PCB.

  6. 06

    STEP and STL exports

    Print-ready STL and STEP files for every part, plus editable source CAD.

Technologies and capabilities

  • STL export

Scope

What is included, and what is not

✓Deliverables

  • 3D enclosure CAD files (editable)
  • STEP files
  • STL files
  • Dimensioned drawings, bill of materials and assembly animation where scoped

✕Not included

  • Enclosure 3D printing or any physical fabrication
  • Mass production and injection-mould tooling
  • Physical product shipping
  • Fit confirmation on a printed part: the first physical print is what confirms fit, and I list what to check

Projects are quoted by scope after I review your requirements. There are no fixed packages.

Do you design custom 3D enclosures?

Yes. I design the enclosure around the actual PCB and your product requirements: mounting points, connector openings, display and button cutouts, with STEP and STL exports. The Beacon enclosure is a five-part example.

Do you provide enclosure 3D printing?

No. I provide print-ready STL and STEP files with suggested print notes. You print the parts yourself or through a printing service of your choice.

Will I receive editable engineering files?

Yes. Depending on the scope, that means the firmware source, wiring diagrams, KiCad schematic and PCB project files, Gerber and drill files, BOM, and the 3D enclosure CAD files with STEP and STL exports, so you are never locked in.
