Service 02

Embedded Firmware Development

Microcontroller firmware for ESP32, STM32 and Arduino products, written in Embedded C/C++ and debugged on real hardware.

Discuss Your ProjectSee what you receive

Overview

Firmware is where your product gets its behaviour. I select a suitable microcontroller for your requirements, write the firmware, and integrate the sensors, actuators and communication modules the product needs.

The firmware is then run on real hardware and debugged there, so by the time the PCB is designed, the behaviour has already been proven.

What you can bring me

  • The product idea or function list
  • Target behaviour and inputs/outputs
  • Preferred microcontroller, if any
  • Sensors and parts you already have
  • Connectivity needs
How it works

What firmware development includes

  1. 01

    Microcontroller programming

    Embedded C/C++ firmware structured around your product requirements, using interrupts, timers and peripheral interfaces where the design calls for them.

  2. 02

    ESP32

    ESP32 family development, including Wi-Fi and Bluetooth/BLE connectivity. I have worked with ESP32-C3, ESP32-S3 and ESP32-C6.

  3. 03

    STM32

    STM32 development using Keil uVision and standard peripherals such as UART, SPI and I2C with interrupt-driven control.

  4. 04

    Arduino

    Arduino-platform development in the Arduino IDE for rapid functional prototypes.

  5. 05

    Sensors

    Reading and conditioning sensor data over I2C, SPI, UART or analogue inputs and turning it into product behaviour.

  6. 06

    Actuators

    Motor, relay and display control, including PWM and driver stages.

  7. 07

    Communication modules

    Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and BLE, and serial links such as UART and USB between the product and other devices.

  8. 08

    Firmware debugging

    Debugging on real hardware: tracing integration problems between firmware and circuit, verifying timing and communication.

Do you develop firmware before PCB design?

Yes. My approach is firmware-first: I make the product work on real hardware before committing to a board. That way the PCB is designed around behaviour that has already been validated, not around assumptions.

Do you provide complete firmware source code?

Yes. For firmware I develop for your product, you receive the complete source code, together with the wiring diagram and testing documentation so the work can be reproduced and extended.

Can you develop an electronic product from just an idea?

Yes. You can start with an idea, a rough sketch, reference images, functional requirements or a full specification. I analyse the requirements, select the microcontroller and components, plan the hardware architecture and then take the product through firmware, breadboard prototype, testing, schematic, PCB layout and enclosure.
