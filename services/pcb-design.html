Service 04

PCB Design

Professional KiCad schematic and PCB engineering, from validated hardware to fabrication-ready files.

Discuss Your ProjectSee what you receive

Overview

Once the functionality is validated, I convert it into a professional schematic and a production-oriented PCB. Component selection, symbols, footprints and the bill of materials are prepared in KiCad, and the layout is checked with electrical and design rules before hand-over.

I also take on PCB work that starts from your finished schematic, an existing board or an open-source design. My largest board so far is an 8-layer, 400+ component multi-radio gateway.

What you can bring me

  • Validated function or a finished schematic
  • Board size, interfaces and key components
  • Layer, cost or manufacturing preferences
  • Your preferred fabricator, if any
How it works

What PCB design includes

  1. 01

    KiCad schematic

    Complete, organised circuit schematic with the electrical connections and a clear block structure.

  2. 02

    Component selection

    Choosing and verifying parts, with specific manufacturer part numbers rather than placeholders.

  3. 03

    Footprints and symbols

    Symbol and footprint management, including custom footprints where a part is not in the library.

  4. 04

    PCB layout

    Board outline and dimensions, layer configuration, component placement and copper zones.

  5. 05

    Routing

    Routing for power, signal and RF where the design needs it, with antenna keep-outs and grounding planned from the first placement.

  6. 06

    Design-rule checks

    Electrical rule checks (ERC) and design rule checks (DRC) run before every hand-over.

  7. 07

    Gerber, drill and BOM

    Gerber files, drill files and a bill of materials, with pick-and-place (CPL) and a STEP model where the project calls for them.

What is included, and what is not

✓Deliverables

  • KiCad schematic files (and PDF)
  • KiCad PCB project files
  • Gerber files
  • Drill files
  • Bill of materials
  • Pick-and-place (CPL) and STEP model where applicable
  • ERC and DRC results

✕Not included

  • Physical PCB manufacturing, printing or assembly (you order boards from the manufacturer you prefer)
  • Component purchasing at my own expense
  • Mass production
  • Physical product shipping
  • Claims of test results on a fabricated board that I have not been given to test

Projects are quoted by scope after I review your requirements. There are no fixed packages.

Do you provide KiCad schematic and PCB files?

Yes. The handover includes the editable KiCad schematic and PCB project, Gerber files, drill files and a bill of materials. KiCad is my default tool. When your project already lives in Altium Designer or EasyEDA Pro, I can work in those as well.

Do you manufacture PCBs?

No. I do not provide PCB manufacturing, printing or assembly. I deliver fabrication-ready files, and you order the boards from the manufacturer of your choice. I have prepared outputs for JLCPCB, PCBWay and OSHPark.

Can you work on existing projects?

Yes. I can join at the stage that fits: layout from a finished schematic (the multi-radio gateway), rebuilding and repairing an undocumented board (ARC62), redesigning a board (RejsaCAN) or designing an enclosure around a supplied PCB.

Need pcb design?

Describe your product or send the requirements. I will review them and come back with the next step.
